# 3. Time and History

The player can choose a starting point in the world's timeline.

## Potentially

- Prehistoric periods, including the age of dinosaurs.
- Ancient civilizations.
- More recent historical periods.
- Modern times.
- The future.
- Potentially geological and astronomical timescales.

History is not a predetermined sequence of events.

The historical timeline is a way to navigate and understand the world's chronology. Civilizations, species, technologies, and events can develop differently depending on the simulation.
