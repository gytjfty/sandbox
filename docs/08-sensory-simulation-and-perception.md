# 8. Sensory Simulation and Perception

The world needs to represent different senses as distinct systems.

- **Sight:** light, color, brightness, shadows, and visual information.
- **Sound:** pitch, frequency, volume, direction, and acoustics.
- **Smell:** chemical compounds and scent perception.
- **Taste:** chemical properties and biological perception.
- **Touch:** pressure, texture, temperature, vibration, and sensation.
- **Balance and spatial awareness.**
- **Internal bodily sensations.**

Different organisms perceive the same environment differently.

Players and NPCs can have different sensory characteristics and conditions, including blindness, deafness, anosmia, peripheral neuropathy, and color vision deficiencies.

These aren't simply arbitrary debuffs. They're part of the underlying biological simulation.
