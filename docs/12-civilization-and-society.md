# 12. Civilization and Society

Civilizations emerge from populations, geography, resources, institutions, and human interactions.

This includes:

- Communities and settlements.
- Villages, cities, nations, and empires.
- Population growth and decline.
- Migration and urbanization.
- Government and political systems.
- Diplomacy and alliances.
- War and territorial conflict.
- Economic activity.
- Culture and social structures.
- Law, crime, and social order.
- Religion, philosophy, and belief systems.

Civilizations can emerge, grow, transform, interact, and disappear.

There are no mandatory historical civilization templates.
