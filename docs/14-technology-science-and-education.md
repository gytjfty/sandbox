# 14. Technology, Science, and Education

Technology should emerge from knowledge, resources, experimentation, and human capabilities.

There shouldn't be a conventional fixed research tree where technologies automatically unlock just because time passes.

People can:

- Experiment independently.
- Study existing discoveries.
- Collaborate with researchers.
- Establish research organizations.
- Invent and develop technologies.
- Share or keep discoveries private.

Education isn't limited to schools.

Learning can happen through experience, observation, mentorship, experimentation, reading, formal education, and collaboration.
