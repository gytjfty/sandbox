# 2. The World

A realistically sized, spherical Earth, more precisely an oblate spheroid.

- The entire planet is represented from the beginning.
- Real-world geography provides the starting foundation.
- Continents, oceans, mountains, coastlines, and geological features can change over time.
- The player can modify terrain at practically any scale, from moving dirt to reshaping continents.
- Underground environments include geological layers, caves, tunnels, and other formations.
- The universe is fictional, but the solar system's planets are based on our own.

Earth is the primary setting, accounting for approximately 99% of gameplay.
