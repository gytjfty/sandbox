# 6. People and NPCs

People are simulated as individuals, not generic characters with a handful of RPG statistics.

Their underlying characteristics can include:

- Biology and anatomy.
- Psychology and personality.
- Intelligence and cognition.
- Knowledge and skills.
- Experiences and memories.
- Relationships and social connections.
- Disabilities, conditions, and individual differences.
- Sensory abilities and limitations.

Not everything needs to be calculated in maximum detail all the time.

## NPC Mode

The player can hand control of their existing character to the NPC AI.

- The AI controls the character autonomously.
- The player can establish broad priorities.
- The AI makes decisions based on circumstances, needs, personality, and available knowledge.
- The player can return after a period of time and discover what happened.

The player and NPCs use the same underlying human simulation.

## Multiplayer

The initial game is single-player, but its underlying design should not unnecessarily prevent future multiplayer support.
