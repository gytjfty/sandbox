# 7. Biology and Ecosystems

Biology is a major part of the simulation.

This includes:

- Individual organisms.
- Animal personalities and behavior.
- Plant biology.
- Reproduction and development.
- Genetics and inheritance.
- Evolution and natural selection.
- Ecosystems and food webs.
- Competition and cooperation.
- Extinction and biodiversity.

Individual animals should have persistent identities rather than existing only as population statistics.

Evolution should emerge from biological processes and environmental circumstances, not predetermined evolutionary paths.

## Extinct Species

The player may be able to preserve or restore extinct species using advanced technology.

This includes scientifically plausible possibilities and more speculative attempts where genetic information is incomplete.

Restored species would interact with the existing world and its ecosystems.
