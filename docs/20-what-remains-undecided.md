# 20. What Remains Undecided?

We've established a surprisingly broad vision, but we haven't finalized everything.

Some major areas still needing exploration include:

- The exact simulation architecture and level-of-detail approach.
- How time acceleration works.
- How distant simulation is maintained.
- The exact depth of human psychology and social behavior.
- The full scope of chemistry and biological simulation.
- The details of multiplayer compatibility.
- The balance between physical accuracy and computational practicality.
- The development priorities and eventual implementation roadmap.

These are not problems we need to solve immediately.

The overall vision: A first-person, planetary-scale, persistent simulation where individuals, organisms, societies, technology, nature, and physical systems all coexist and influence one another. The player is free to participate in this world however they want, while the world continues to evolve independently.

The central ambition isn't to create a game with an enormous number of features. It's to create a world where those features actually interact and produce outcomes we didn't explicitly script.
