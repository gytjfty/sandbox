# 10. Chemistry and Materials

Chemistry is an underlying foundation of the world.

Potential scope includes:

- The periodic table and chemical elements.
- Compounds and mixtures.
- Material composition and properties.
- Chemical reactions.
- States of matter.
- Corrosion, combustion, decomposition, and other processes.

Materials should have meaningful physical and chemical properties.

The goal isn't to simulate every atom. It's to make chemistry matter to the systems that depend on it.
