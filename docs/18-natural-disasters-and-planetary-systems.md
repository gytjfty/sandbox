# 18. Natural Disasters and Planetary Systems

Natural events emerge from the physical simulation.

Examples include:

- Earthquakes.
- Volcanic eruptions.
- Hurricanes and tornadoes.
- Floods and droughts.
- Landslides.
- Wildfires.
- Tsunamis.
- Meteor impacts.

These events can cause cascading consequences for populations, infrastructure, ecosystems, and economies.

## Oceans and Marine Environments

Oceans are complete simulated environments, not just large bodies of water on the map.

They include marine organisms, ecosystems, currents, seafloor geology, chemistry, and human interaction.

## Astronomy and Planetary Systems

The game also accounts for:

- Day and night.
- Seasons.
- Orbits and gravity.
- Lunar phases and tides.
- Eclipses.
- Solar activity.
- Other planets and moons.
- Long-term planetary changes.

These affect the simulated world rather than merely decorating the sky.
