# 13. Economics, Taxation, and Trade

Economics is a major interconnected system.

- Production and consumption.
- Businesses and industries.
- Resources and commodities.
- Trade and transportation.
- Supply and demand.
- Employment and livelihoods.
- Wealth and economic inequality.
- Government budgets and taxation.
- Economic growth and decline.
- Recessions and other emergent economic outcomes.

Large populations can be represented through aggregated economic activity, while individuals and organizations still participate in the system.

Governments have actual financial constraints. Taxes, spending, and economic policies have consequences.
