# 9. Weather, Climate, and Water

## Weather and Climate

Multiple levels of simulation:

- Global atmospheric circulation.
- Regional weather systems.
- Local weather conditions.
- Storms, precipitation, temperature, wind, humidity, and other phenomena.

Physically grounded simulation where practical, with mathematical approximations where necessary.

## Water

Water should be physically interactive and preferably volumetric.

It can occupy space, flow, interact with terrain and objects, fill underground spaces, and affect the environment.

Water is connected to geology, weather, ecosystems, chemistry, and infrastructure.
