# 15. Communication and Language

Communication is a major system in its own right.

It includes:

- Speech and spoken languages.
- Writing and literacy.
- Sign languages and gestures.
- Facial expressions and other nonverbal communication.
- Animal communication.
- Long-distance communication technologies.
- Information networks.
- News, rumors, misinformation, and propaganda.

Languages can emerge, evolve, split into dialects, spread, and disappear.

Information doesn't magically reach everyone. It has to travel through actual communication systems.
