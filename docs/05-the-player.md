# 5. The Player

- Entirely first-person.
- Starts as an ordinary individual within the simulation.
- Survival is governed by the same underlying rules as everyone else.
- Survival is important, but isn't necessarily the player's primary objective.
- The player can pursue practically any goal.
- Biological aging can potentially be overcome through advanced technology.
- Immortality is possible as a simulated outcome, not guaranteed invulnerability.
- The player can potentially transition into biomechanical, mechanical, or computerized forms.

The player isn't a special entity with an entirely separate simulation model.
