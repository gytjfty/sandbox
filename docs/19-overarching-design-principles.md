# 19. The Overarching Design Principles

These are arguably the most important parts of the entire project.

1. **Emergence over scripting:** Events should arise from underlying systems.
2. **Interconnectedness:** Systems influence one another rather than operating in isolation.
3. **Consistent abstraction:** Detailed and simplified simulation must agree with each other.
4. **Individuality:** People and animals should have meaningful individual differences.
5. **Persistence:** The world continues existing beyond the player's immediate observation.
6. **Freedom:** The player should be able to experiment and pursue unusual goals.
7. **Scientific grounding:** Real-world science provides a foundation, with room for internally consistent speculation.
8. **Scalability:** The simulation must balance detail, performance, and scope.
9. **No artificial protagonist privilege:** The player operates under the same world rules as everyone else.
