# 1. The Core Idea

A massive, open-ended, first-person simulation of an entire planet.

The player exists inside a persistent, interconnected world where practically anything can happen. It isn't primarily a survival game, RPG, city builder, or conventional open-world game.

It's a world simulation first, with gameplay emerging from the simulation.

## Core Principles

- **Very low scriptedness.**
- **Highly interconnected systems.**
- **Emergent events and outcomes.**
- **Individual and large-scale simulation.**
- **Freedom to experiment, build, explore, modify, and pursue personal goals.**
- **The world continues existing independently of the player.**

The world doesn't revolve around the player.
