# 11. Construction, Engineering, and Creation

The player has a separate creative/design mode resembling a 3D editor.

- Arbitrary dimensions and geometry.
- Custom objects, buildings, machines, and structures.
- Designs exist as data or blueprints.
- Designs can be physically realized through manufacturing, materials, tools, and technology.

A designed machine should actually function within the simulation.

NPCs can also independently develop designs, invent things, manufacture objects, and share knowledge.
