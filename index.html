ATLAS / BOUND ORBITS, FLYBYS & ESCAPE

The physical player now uses a universal-variable state propagator for bound, near-parabolic and unbound stellar trajectories. Passing escape energy no longer rejects an otherwise supported velocity change. The HUD explicitly names the trajectory and its one reference star. No automatic gravitational handoffs.

NEW: Local Gravity tab adds a separate numerical player mode pulled by the system's star and planets. Original Explorer and single-star Player Flight remain available. Planet masses are synthetic density proxies; planetary orbits remain prescribed. No terrain or survival systems added.

Player Flight adds a timestamped maneuver journal with RTN and inertial Δv, before/after velocities and resulting trajectory classes. Preview never applies a change. Bound paths display a closed orbit when the period is numerically practical; open and very-long-period paths display a labeled forward prediction window.

Physical controls: W/S ±transverse, D/A ±radial, E/Q ±normal; one ideal impulse per new press. Comma/period change pulse size, not velocity. No instant brake, finite engine/fuel model or physical autopilot. Mouse only changes view. The player and planets share one simulation clock; pausing freezes coast, not an explicitly requested impulse.

Player reference is one fixed star with μ=G Mstar. No planet gravity, N-body perturbations, stellar recoil, relativity or collisions. Star-intersecting conics and degenerate radial states remain explicitly rejected. Far from the star, this is still the same single-star approximation; entering a display region never changes the force model.

Old fixed-star-player-v1 checkpoints are verified and migrated on restore to a Cartesian seed at their saved conic epoch. Their earlier maneuver history cannot be recovered and is not invented. Saving writes universal-player-v2 in the same per-session player key; older releases cannot read the new format. Export the old checkpoint before replacing it if you need rollback.

Explorer, physical player and world edits keep independent save contracts. K saves world + active navigation mode only. Returning to Explorer changes neither live player trajectory nor stored checkpoint. Restoring player JSON explicitly restores the shared clock. Existing v4/v5 population and planar/spatial namespaces remain intact.

Internet required for Three.js. NULL needs Blob workers, pointer lock and local storage.

PROPAGATION AND PREDICTION

     
Player motion uses nondimensionalized universal variables, Stumpff functions and a bracketed Newton/bisection root solve. It propagates Cartesian position/velocity without selecting separate elliptic/hyperbolic formulas. Planet motion remains the prior basis-vector bound Kepler model. Signed player energy is not clamped at the escape boundary.

     
JOURNAL AND CHECKPOINT MIGRATION

     
New player v2 records a Cartesian initialization seed, ordered applied maneuvers with RTN/inertial Δv and before/after states, and a shared-clock checkpoint.
